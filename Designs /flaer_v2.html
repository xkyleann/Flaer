How it works
01
Map your infrastructure

Add owned, leased, or colocated data centers in minutes. Flaer pulls live grid carbon intensity from Electricity Maps and WattTime — updated every five minutes, automatically.

02
See your 10-year trajectory

Our ML model, trained on SSP2 and SSP5-8.5 climate scenarios, forecasts energy demand and carbon emissions at each facility through 2035. Know what is coming before it arrives.

03

Flaer surfaces your three highest-ROI actions — workload shifting, cooling optimisation, renewable switching — with exact CO₂ and cost impact. Not a dashboard. A decision engine.
