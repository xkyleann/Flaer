How it works

From invisible
to actionable.

Three steps from sign-up to your first recommendation.

01
Map your infrastructure

Add your data centers in minutes. Flaer pulls live carbon intensity from Electricity Maps and WattTime, updated every five minutes. No manual data entry.

Our ML model — trained on SSP2 and SSP5-8.5 climate scenarios — forecasts energy demand and carbon emissions at every facility through 2035.
