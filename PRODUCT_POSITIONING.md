# Flaer — Product Positioning & Competitive Differentiation

> *The sustainability intelligence platform built specifically for data center operators and planners.*

---

## The Problem

Data center operators face three converging pressures:

- **€110/tCO₂e** carbon costs projected under EU ETS by 2030
- **Mandatory CSRD disclosure** for any large company operating in the EU (FY2024+)
- **Board-level net-zero commitments** with no credible pathway to report against

Their current tooling leaves a critical gap:

| Tool Category | Examples | What They Do | What They Miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generic ESG platforms | Watershed, Persefoni, Salesforce Net Zero Cloud | Scope 1/2/3 accounting | No DC-specific metrics — no PUE, WUE, CUE, grid carbon intensity, cooling efficiency |
| DCIM / operational tools | Nlyte, Sunbird, Vertiv | Uptime, capacity, power monitoring | No carbon forecasting, no CSRD output, no site planning |
| Manual spreadsheets | Excel, Google Sheets | Whatever you build | No AI, no real-time data, no scenario modelling, no audit trail |

**No existing tool combines operational sustainability intelligence with new facility planning for data centers. That is Flaer's position.**

---

## What Flaer Does

Flaer is a single platform that serves two workflows that have never been connected before:

### 1. Operators — Running today's facilities

- Real-time PUE, WUE, CUE monitoring with anomaly detection
- Scope 1, 2 & 3 carbon accounting (GHG Protocol aligned)
- CSRD / EU Taxonomy compliance dashboard — export-ready
- Emissions forecasting under SSP5-8.5, balanced, and custom climate scenarios
- Weekly AI-generated consumption digest with ranked reduction actions
- Facility-vs-facility comparison across all sustainability dimensions

### 2. Planners — Deciding where to build next

- 14-dimension MCDA site scoring (carbon, water, climate, regulatory, financial, infrastructure)
- Phase-gate workflow: Screening → Pre-Feasibility → Risk Assessment → Financial Model
- 5×5 risk matrix with mitigation plans per candidate site
- CapEx · OpEx · IRR · NPV · payback financial model
- Gantt timeline from permitting to commissioning
- Due diligence report generation (one click, PDF)
- Shortlist management across candidate locations

## Key Differentiators

### 1. Data Center Native
Generic ESG tools require months of custom configuration to understand what a PUE of 1.47 means, or why a 408 gCO₂/kWh grid makes Singapore a CSRD liability. Flaer is pre-configured with DC-specific thresholds, benchmarks, and terminology on day one.

### 2. Operator + Planner in One Platform
No competitor serves both the sustainability manager optimising today's fleet and the infrastructure team evaluating next year's build in the same product. Flaer connects operational data directly into site selection scoring — a Stockholm expansion scores higher partly because your existing Nordic facility proves the operational model works.

### 3. CSRD Out of the Box
EU Taxonomy alignment criteria, Scope 2 market-based vs location-based accounting, and CSRD disclosure templates are built into the core data model — not a paid add-on configured by a consultant over six months.

### 4. Site Intelligence at Engineering Grade
The Site IQ screen goes beyond a scoring table. It generates the same artefacts a project team would produce manually over weeks: MCDA weighted analysis, risk register, permitting timeline, financial model, and a structured due diligence report — in minutes.

### 5. AI with Portfolio Context
The flaer AI assistant (powered by Llama 3.3 70B via Groq) has access to the user's actual portfolio — PUE, WUE, carbon intensity, and capacity per facility. It does not give generic answers. It tells you specifically that your N. Virginia facility's 61% renewable mix is your largest Scope 2 exposure and estimates the exact saving from a PPA renegotiation.

### 6. Climate Scenario Forecasting
Flaer shows boards and sustainability committees exactly what happens under SSP5-8.5 high-warming vs an optimised pathway — with adjustable levers for renewable procurement, PUE improvement, and workload shift. No other DC tool does this.

## What to Build Next (Priority Order)

1. **Automated CSRD report export** — full PDF disclosure ready for audit. Saves €30–50k in consulting fees per customer per year. Highest willingness-to-pay.
2. **Real-time grid carbon API** — live carbon intensity per region (Electricity Maps integration). Enables workload shift recommendations with actual timing.
3. **Cloud provider carbon benchmarking** — pull AWS, Azure, GCP region-level carbon data. Tells customers where to run workloads, not just where to build.
4. **Benchmark database** — anonymised PUE/WUE/CUE by region and cooling type. Network effect kicks in at ~50 customers.
5. **Scope 3 supply chain module** — Category 11 (use of sold products) and Category 1 (purchased goods/services). Completes the GHG Protocol picture.
